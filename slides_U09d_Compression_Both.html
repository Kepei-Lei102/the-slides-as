One is a promise. One is a judgement.

Lossless

The original data can be recovered exactly.

A changed digit in an account is not “close enough”.

Lossy

Some original information is permanently lost.

A smaller listening copy may be useful even though it differs from the recording.

Saving a damaged copy at a higher quality setting does not restore the missing original.

📝 IN THE EXAMLossy removes information permanently; lossless allows exact reconstruction.
🏛 IN REALITYA format may offer both methods. Choose according to the use and the actual encoding.
