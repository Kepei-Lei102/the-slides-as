Why the negative cancels you to zero

A four-bit wheel has 16 positions. Adding 1 to 1111 leaves 0000 in the four cells.
The pattern for−5 is 11:5+11=16, so the low four bits return to 0.
The carry drops off; signed 5+(−5)=0 still fits. That carry is not signed overflow.

Overflow Hall of Fame

👾 Pac-Man

At level 256, a wrapped counter in the fruit-drawing routine makes it draw beyond its intended data. A small integer becomes a screen-sized mess.

🚀 Ariane 501

A failed conversion in the guidance system triggered a malfunction response. Both reference systems failed; handling the error was part of the disaster.

🎬 Gangnam Style

The famous 2,147,483,647 boundary became a capacity-planning story. YouTube said its 64-bit upgrade had already happened; the “broken counter” story was a joke.

⏰2038

A signed 32-bit seconds-since 1970 field has an end date. Wider time fields exist; the risk belongs to systems and formats still using the narrow field.

AS 9618

BCD: a label for each decimal digit

59 as binary: 0011 1011 59 as BCD: 0101 1001 5 9 1010 is not a decimal digit.
A BCD-to-seven-segment decoder accepts four bits for a decimal digit and drives the display segments.
Example: TI’s CD4511B displays codes 0…9; codes above 9 blank its output.
BCD stores digits. A decimal point, scale, rounding rule and overflow policy still need defining.
📝 IN THE EXAMAS: recall BCD conversions and describe practical applications. BCD is not on the IG route.
🏛 IN REALITYDisplays can use BCD interfaces; not every display or money program does. Scaled binary integers can also store exact cents.
